Protect your Jeep's suspension from over-extension on the trail. The best limit strap brands for JK, JL, JT, and more.

Why Jeeps Need Limit Straps

Jeep solid-axle suspensions are designed for maximum articulation — but that flexibility comes with a cost. Without limit straps, full droop can over-extend shocks, stress brake lines, and put dangerous loads on suspension components. This is especially true on lifted Jeeps with longer travel where the factory bump stops no longer control extension.
