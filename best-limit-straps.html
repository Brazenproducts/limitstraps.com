How to Choose the Right Limit Strap

  • Material: Look for military-spec nylon or polyester webbing with reinforced stitching.
  • Length: Measure your suspension's full droop travel and add 1–2 inches.
  • Width: 1.5" is standard for most applications. 2" for heavier vehicles or race use.
  • Hardware: Grade 8 bolts and reinforced mounting tabs matter as much as the strap.
  • Made in USA: Domestically manufactured straps use better materials and tighter QC.
