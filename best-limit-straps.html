How to Choose the Right Limit Strap

When picking a limit strap, here's what matters most:

  • Material: Look for military-spec nylon or polyester webbing with reinforced stitching. Cheap straps fray and snap under hard use.
  • Length: Measure your suspension's full droop travel and add 1–2 inches. Too short = restricted travel. Too long = no protection.
  • Width: 1.5" is standard for most applications. 2" for heavier vehicles or race use.
  • Hardware: Quality bolt-on mounting hardware matters as much as the strap itself. Look for grade 8 bolts and reinforced mounting tabs.
  • Made in USA: Off-road parts take serious abuse. Domestically manufactured straps from companies like Bull Strap use better materials and tighter quality control than imports.
