Why Trucks Need Limit Straps

Truck suspensions — especially independent front suspension (IFS) — are vulnerable to over-extension damage:

  • CV axle protection: Over-extension stretches CV boots and damages joints
  • Ball joint preservation: Upper and lower ball joints wear fast without travel limits
  • Shock protection: Prevents shocks from bottoming out on extension
  • Consistent handling: Keeps suspension geometry predictable during hard off-road use

Any truck running a lift, long-travel kit, or seeing serious off-road use should have limit straps. They're cheap insurance against expensive suspension repairs.
