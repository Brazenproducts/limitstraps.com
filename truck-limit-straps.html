Why Trucks Need Limit Straps

  • CV axle protection: Over-extension stretches CV boots and damages joints
  • Ball joint preservation: Upper and lower ball joints wear fast without travel limits
  • Shock protection: Prevents shocks from bottoming out on extension
  • Consistent handling: Keeps geometry predictable during hard off-road use
