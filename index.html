Your Guide to Made in USA Suspension Limit Straps

Limit straps protect your suspension from over-extension, preventing costly damage to shocks, axles, and CV joints. The right straps can save you thousands in repairs. We've tested and reviewed the top brands so you don't have to guess.

What Are Limit Straps?

Limit straps are heavy-duty webbing straps that bolt between your vehicle's frame and axle (or suspension arm) to limit how far the suspension can droop. Without them, full extension can destroy shocks, tear CV boots, and snap brake lines. They're cheap insurance for expensive components.

Why Limit Straps Matter

Protect Your Shocks

Over-extension is the #1 killer of off-road shocks. Limit straps prevent your suspension from exceeding its designed travel, keeping shocks within their operating range.

Save Thousands in Repairs

A set of limit straps costs a fraction of what it takes to replace blown shocks, torn CV boots, or snapped brake lines. It's the cheapest protection you can add to your rig.
